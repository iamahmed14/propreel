Import button for your browser

If a portal blocks link import, open the listing in your own browser and tap this button. It reads the page you're looking at and sends it here.

Computer: drag this to your bookmarks bar: ＋ PropReel

Phone: bookmark any page, edit the bookmark and replace its address with the copied code. On a listing, type "PropReel" in the address bar and tap the bookmark.
